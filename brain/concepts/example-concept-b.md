## What it is

*Linking* connects one note to another with a `[[wikilink]]`. Each link is also a
**backlink** on the target, and every link is an edge in the graph view. Over time the
links — not folders — become the primary structure of the vault.

## Why it matters

Links turn a pile of notes into a navigable network: you reach related ideas by following
connections instead of remembering where a file lives. The graph view surfaces clusters and
isolated (orphan) notes so you can see where knowledge is dense or thin.

## Key facts

- A `[[wikilink]]` creates a clickable link *and* a backlink on the target. Source: example placeholder.
- Links work best between [[example-concept-a]] — small, single-idea notes — because the connection is precise. Source: example placeholder.
- The graph view renders every link as an edge; orphan notes (no links) stand out visually. Source: example placeholder.

## How to apply it

- When you mention another concept, link it with `[[...]]` rather than restating it.
- Check the Backlinks panel to discover unexpected related notes.
- Anti-pattern: relying only on folders — folders don't show relationships, links do.
